# Evo 4

Evo 4 begins as a design and project-archaeology effort, not an assumption that the next implementation should immediately begin.

## Working method

- Treat prior projects as evidence: distinguish stated intent, implemented behavior, tests, and behavior actually verified.
- Preserve important decisions and their rationale as the project evolves.
- Keep unresolved questions visible instead of silently turning guesses into requirements.
- Record rejected options and why they were rejected, so they are not repeatedly reconsidered without new evidence.
- Do not claim tests, capabilities, or repository findings that have not been checked.
